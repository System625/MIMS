# Listing images — provenance and licence

**These are stock photographs of the KIND of part, not photographs of the item
we would ship.** They are here by an explicit founder decision on 2026-09-12,
taken with the trade-off understood: see `bella.md` §3 and §10, and the
`illustrative` flag in `apps/web/src/mock/listings.ts` that makes every one of
them label itself on the page.

All four are from Pexels under the [Pexels
License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) — free for commercial use, no
attribution required. Attribution is recorded here anyway, because a licence
that does not require a record is not a reason to keep none: if any of these
ever has to be traced, replaced or defended, this file is the trail.

## Replacing these

They are a stopgap and should be replaced by photographs of our own held stock
the moment those exist. When they are, drop the `illustrative` flag on the entry
in `PART_PHOTOS` — the frame switches from a cropped illustrative plate to a
contained product shot and the "not the item" labelling disappears, because it
will no longer be true.

## What must never go in here

An image showing a manufacturer's badge or wordmark, on any listing that is not
that manufacturer's own part. A Toyota roundel on an aftermarket bumper is a
brand claim as well as a misdescription, and it is the one version of this
shortcut that turns a consumer-law problem into a trademark one.
